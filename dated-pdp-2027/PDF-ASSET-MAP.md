## Storefront preparation

- Keep the source PDFs as print masters. Do not upload full PDFs as storefront media.
- Export the selected pages/spreads as sRGB WebP or high-quality JPEG, approximately 1800-2200 px on the long edge.
- Remove print bleed and crop marks from storefront exports.
- Build one clean cover mockup, one close-up of binding/material, and one scale-in-hand image when physical samples are available.
- Use concise alt text that names the feature shown, for example: `2027 dated planner monthly calendar spread`.
- Keep cover choice and personalisation separate in the product form. A 40-cover selector would be too dense for one PDP; use separate products or a much smaller curated cover family.
