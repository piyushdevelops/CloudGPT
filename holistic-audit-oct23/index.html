What passed

  • Draft status confirmed through Shopify; MAIN is a different theme.
  • 18 sampled routes opened at 393×852 and 1440×1000, covering homepage, countdown, five collections, four products and seven supporting/policy pages. Initial viewport geometry checks found no horizontal page overflow. Loaded-image checks found no broken images in that sample; this is not a guarantee about every lazy-loaded asset.
  • Desktop predictive search returned relevant planners. Mobile menu opened and Escape closed it.
  • Collection price sorting changed the product order; filter drawer opened and closed; Load more increased cards from 24 to 48.
  • Alpine Timber Glass added at ₹299. Drawer quantity 2 produced ₹598 subtotal and ₹1,400 comparison savings.
  • GoKwik opened with 2 items and ₹598. No login, OTP or purchase performed.
  • Side drawer removal reached the empty state immediately. Both audit products were removed; audit-session cart restored to zero.
  • Countdown displays 26 October 2026, 5pm IST. After reveal, no green asterisk emoji was found in rendered text. At the page bottom, document height was 1,631px and footer bottom 1,630.78px: no extra document area below the footer in this browser.

Coverage and limits

This is a representative storefront audit, not certification of every product, variant or external service. Page captures include early loading states; use settled captures and the findings above rather than treating every initially blank image as a defect.

Still required: physical iPhone Safari scroll/keyboard testing, Android testing, real-device reduced-motion and touch checks, a completed authorised test order including discounts/shipping/refunds, sold-out and no-sale variants, bundle contents and properties, custom-name persistence after repair, analytics event deduplication, account/order tracking, newsletter delivery, full keyboard/screen-reader and colour-contrast testing, and measured Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals. Performance has not been scored. The launch countdown expiry transition has not been simulated.
