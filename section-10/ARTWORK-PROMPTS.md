Built-in ImageGen. Reconstructed views based on catalog images, not original product photography. Three individual pieces; not a bundled set.

## Asset 0
Product-mockup cutout for an interactive dining stack. Reference image shows the real product: single glossy pink ceramic bowl, diameter 11cm height 6cm. Remove every other bowl and plate; preserve rounded plain bowl with thin gold lip. Reconstruct ONLY this one object in a consistent front three-quarter camera view elevated 30 degrees above tabletop, centered, horizontal rim ellipse. Preserve its pink color and simple smooth geometry; no scalloping, no patterns. Soft studio light upper left. Isolated on genuinely transparent alpha background, no floor, no cast shadow, no props, no text, no checkerboard. Landscape canvas, object fills 85% width with generous vertical transparent margins. Premium realistic catalog rendering.
