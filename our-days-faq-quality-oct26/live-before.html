Good to know

Questions, answered.

What if I miss a prompt?+

Nothing is lost. Open your private timeline to answer it later, skip it, or add a different memory from that day.

Can two parents contribute?+

The pilot begins with one primary WhatsApp number. A second contributor is planned for the self-service release.

Can I change the book before printing?+

Yes. Edit text, crop or replace photos, change dates, exclude memories and review the complete proof before approval.

What happens after I choose a plan?+

Complete checkout, then we’ll guide you through the recipient, start date and prompt preferences for your chapter.
